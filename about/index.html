Built by someone who understands both the work and the systems behind it.

The honest version

I didn't start with marketing. I started by solving operational problems.

Before building websites and CRMs, I worked on the operations side — project management, estimating, documentation, and technical work in engineering and construction environments. That's where I saw the real problem up close.

Businesses rarely lose customers because their service is bad. They lose them because information gets scattered. A quotation sits unanswered. A follow-up slips through a busy week. Details live in three notebooks, two spreadsheets, and someone's Messenger inbox. Repetitive tasks eat the hours that should go into actual work.

Technology is supposed to make that easier. Too often it makes it more complicated — another app, another login, another thing to maintain.

I now help businesses build simple systems that connect the important parts of their customer journey: the website that gets them found, the CRM that keeps every inquiry organized, and the automation that makes sure follow-up actually happens.

What that background means for you

  • Systems thinking — from engineering and estimating work: every build is scoped, documented, and testable.
  • Operations first — the system fits how you actually work, not the other way around.
  • Plain explanations — you'll understand what was built and why, without needing to learn jargon.
