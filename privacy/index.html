Privacy Policy

Plain language, as required by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

Last updated: [DATE — set before launch]

What we collect

When you submit a form on this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, business name, mobile number, email address, website or Facebook Page (if given), business type, and your message. We also collect standard analytics data (pages visited, device type, and how you found us) through Google Analytics using cookies.

Why we collect it

We use your contact details for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry, provide the audit or consultation you requested, and follow up about that request. Analytics data helps us understand which pages are useful so we can improve the website.

What we don't do

We do not sell your information. We do not add you to marketing campaigns without telling you. Follow-up messages relate to the inquiry you made, and you can ask us to stop at any time by replying "stop" or emailing us.

Where your data lives

Inquiry data is stored in our customer relationship management (CRM) system and email. Access is limited to the people who handle your inquiry.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask us what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@yourdomain.ph and we'll act on it promptly.

Cookies

This site uses analytics cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.
