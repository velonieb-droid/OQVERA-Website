FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

I already have a Facebook Page. Do I still need a website?

A Facebook Page is a good start, but you don't control it — reach changes, posts get buried, and it's hard for customers to see your full services or send a structured inquiry. A website gives you a home base that shows up on Google, works with a proper inquiry form, and connects to your CRM. Most of our clients keep both: Facebook for conversations, the website for search and structured leads.

I'm a small business. Do I really need a CRM?

If you get more than a handful of inquiries a week and you're tracking them in your head, a notebook, or a spreadsheet, then yes — a simple one. A CRM just keeps every inquiry, conversation, and follow-up in one place so nothing gets forgotten. We set it up simply. You don't need to use every feature on day one.

Can you connect Facebook inquiries?

Yes. Messenger conversations can flow into the same inbox and CRM as your website inquiries, so you reply from one place and every lead is tracked the same way.

Can you work with my existing website?

Usually, yes. If your current site is in decent shape, we can connect forms, add booking, and wire it to a CRM without rebuilding. If it needs a rebuild, we'll tell you honestly and explain why.

Do I need to change my current system?

Not necessarily. We start by understanding how you work today. Where a tool is doing its job, we keep it. We only replace things that are actively costing you leads or time.

Can you manage the website after launch?

Yes. We offer hosting, maintenance, updates, and ongoing support so you don't need to handle the technical side yourself.

Can I start with only a website?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a website, then add CRM, booking, and automation later once inquiries pick up. You don't have to buy everything at once.

Can you automate customer follow-up?

Yes — polite, well-timed follow-ups by email or SMS after an inquiry or quotation, and reminders before appointments. The automation stops the moment the customer books or replies, so it never feels robotic.

How much does it cost?

Starter begins at ₱9,900/month, Growth at ₱19,900/month, and Pro/Automation at ₱34,900/month — each with a one-time setup fee. See the full breakdown on our Pricing page, or book a free consultation and we'll recommend the right starting point.

What happens if I stop the monthly service?

Your website and your customer data are yours. If you pause ongoing support, we hand everything over cleanly — access, files, and export of your CRM data. No lock-in.
