FAQ

Pricing questions, answered honestly

Why is there a range for the setup fee?

The monthly price is fixed per package, but setup scope still varies a little — a 5-page site is less work than a 10-page site with more integrations. After a free consultation you get a written quote with an exact number inside that range — no surprises.

Do I have to buy everything at once?

No. Most clients start with a website, then add CRM, then automation, then SEO, then ongoing support — in that order, when each one makes sense. That upgrade path is deliberate.

Is there a lock-in contract?

Project work is one-time. Ongoing services (hosting, maintenance, support) are month-to-month. If you stop, your website and your data are handed over cleanly.

What happens if I stop the monthly service?

Your website and customer data are yours. We hand over access, files and a full CRM export. Nothing is held hostage.
