Plain-language answer

What is a CRM?

A CRM, or Customer Relationship Management system, is a tool that helps businesses organize customer information, inquiries, follow-ups, appointments and sales activity in one place. For a small business, it simply means: every lead is recorded, every conversation has context, and every follow-up has a date — instead of living in someone's head.

FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

I'm a small business. Do I really need a CRM?

If you get more than a handful of inquiries a week and follow-up depends on memory — yes, a simple one. If you get two inquiries a month, honestly, you may not need one yet, and we'll tell you that.

Which CRM do you use?

We work with proven small-business platforms and set up the one that fits your needs and budget. You get the login, and the data is always yours.

Can it capture leads from Facebook?

Yes. Messenger conversations and lead forms can flow into the same pipeline as website inquiries, so everything is tracked one way.

Will my team actually use it?

That's the real question, and it's why we keep the setup minimal: one pipeline, clear stages, mobile access, and short training. Complexity is what kills CRM adoption — so we leave it out.

What happens to my data if I stop using it?

We export everything — contacts, notes, history — and hand it to you. Your customer list is your asset, not ours.
