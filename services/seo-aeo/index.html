What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO means structuring your website so that AI assistants and answer engines — like chat-based search — can understand what you do, where you operate, and answer customers' questions with your business as the source. In practice: clear headings, direct plain-language answers, accurate structured data, and consistent business information across the web.

FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

How long until SEO shows results?

Local improvements (Maps, Business Profile) can move within weeks. Competitive rankings typically take a few months of consistent work. Anyone promising page one in two weeks is guessing or gaming — and we're neither.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest provider can — Google decides rankings, not us. We guarantee the work: proper structure, real content, accurate data, and transparent monthly reporting on movement.

Is AEO worth it for a small business?

Increasingly, yes. More customers ask AI assistants for recommendations, and the businesses with clearly structured, answer-shaped content are the ones that get cited. The good news: AEO work also improves regular SEO.

Do I need new content?

Usually some. Pages that directly answer your customers' real questions are what rank and get cited. We write them with you, in plain language — no keyword-stuffed filler.

Can you do SEO for my Facebook Page instead of a website?

Facebook Pages have very limited search presence. For Google and AI search you need a website — even a lean one — plus an optimized Business Profile. We can start there.
