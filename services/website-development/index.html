FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

How long does a website take to build?

Most small-business websites take a few weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly content and photos come together. You'll get a specific timeline in your quote.

Can you work with my existing website?

Usually yes. If the foundation is sound we connect forms, booking and CRM to what you have. If a rebuild would genuinely serve you better, we'll say so and explain why.

Do I need to write the content myself?

No. We draft the pages with you based on how you actually talk to customers, then you review and approve everything.

Will I be able to update it myself?

Yes — we build on platforms you can edit, and we show you how. If you'd rather not touch it, our maintenance service handles updates for you.

Is a website really better than just boosting Facebook posts?

They do different jobs. Boosted posts create attention; a website catches the people who search for your service on Google and gives them a structured way to inquire. Most businesses need both working together.
