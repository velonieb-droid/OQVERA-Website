Plain-language answer

Can Facebook inquiries be automated without annoying customers?

Yes — if automation handles acknowledgment and timing, not the whole conversation. An instant 'Got your message, we'll reply shortly' with answers to common questions feels responsive; a bot that traps people in menus feels terrible. The rule: automate the first ten seconds, then hand off to a human.
