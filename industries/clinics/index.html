FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

Is patient information safe in a system like this?

Patient data handling follows the Data Privacy Act: consent collected properly, access limited, communications only for legitimate care-related purposes. We set up the consent language with you and keep clinical records where they belong — the CRM handles contact, scheduling and communication.

Can patients still book by phone or walk in?

Of course. Online booking adds a channel; it doesn't remove any. Walk-ins and phone bookings get logged in the same calendar so nothing double-books.

We use a queueing / HMO process. Does this conflict?

No — we map the system around your actual intake process. Booking can gather HMO details upfront so the front desk isn't collecting them at the counter.

Will reminders and recalls feel spammy to patients?

Reminders about their own appointments are the messages patients appreciate most. Recalls go only to consenting patients, at sensible intervals, and stop on request — always.

What's your no-show rate this month?

If you don't know the number, that's the first thing the free consultation will find. Then we'll fix it.
