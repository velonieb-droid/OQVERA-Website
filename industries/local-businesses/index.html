FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

My business is small. Isn't this overkill?

The setup scales to the business. For many local businesses, the right start is just an optimized Google profile, a one-page site, and Messenger auto-answers — modest cost, immediate difference. Add more only if growth demands it.

Do I need a website if I'm mostly a walk-in business?

A simple one, yes — because 'search first, visit second' is now how walk-ins happen. Even one great page with accurate info outperforms no presence.

Who updates all this? I don't have staff for it.

That's what hosting and maintenance covers — we make the updates, keep info accurate, and stay reachable. You run the store.

Can I message my customers about promos?

Yes — customers who opted in. We build the consent step in from the start, per the Data Privacy Act, so your list is both useful and proper.
