FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

I get leads from many sources. Can they all go to one place?

Yes — website, Facebook, and manually-logged portal or referral leads all land in one pipeline, each tagged by source so you know what's producing.

Won't months of automated messages annoy prospects?

Not if they're spaced sensibly and actually useful — a relevant new listing, a market update. That's staying helpful, not spamming. And every message has an easy opt-out.

Can my team of agents share the system?

Yes — leads can route to agents, each sees their own pipeline, and you see the whole picture. No more leads lost in someone's personal Messenger.

Does this work for pre-selling projects with long timelines?

It's built for exactly that. Milestone updates, payment reminders where appropriate, and consistent contact across a multi-year timeline are what the system does best.

How many of last year's 'not yet' leads are buying right now?

Someone is closing them. A free consultation shows you the follow-up system that makes it you.
