The industry reality

A contractor's leads are big, slow — and easy to lose.

A homeowner inquires about a renovation. You're on site all day. By the time you reply, they've messaged three other contractors. Weeks pass between site visit, estimate and decision — and if follow-up depends on memory, the project quietly goes to whoever stayed in touch.

FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

My projects come from referrals. Why do I need this?

Referrals still search your name before calling — and finding nothing (or an abandoned Page) costs trust. And the system's biggest value for referral businesses is follow-up: making sure the estimates you already send don't die in silence.

Clients inquire on Facebook. Can that connect?

Yes. Messenger inquiries land in the same pipeline as website quote requests, get the same instant acknowledgment, and get tracked the same way.

Estimates take me days to prepare. Does automation still help?

Especially then. The system acknowledges the inquiry instantly and keeps the client warm while you prepare properly — 'estimate in progress' beats silence.

Can it handle both residential and commercial inquiries?

Yes — the quote form can route by project type, so a condo renovation and a warehouse fit-out enter different pipeline paths with different follow-up.
