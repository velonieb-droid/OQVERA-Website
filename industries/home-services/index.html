FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

My schedule changes daily. Can booking handle that?

Yes — booking reads your real availability, and urgent jobs can still come in as quote requests you confirm manually. You choose the mix.

Most customers just call. Is this still useful?

Calls are exactly why missed-call text-back exists — it converts the calls you physically can't answer. And a growing share of customers, especially younger ones, prefer to book without calling at all.

Can it track which customer had which service?

Yes — every customer record keeps address, units serviced, and job history, so repeat visits and warranty questions have context instantly.

Will review requests annoy customers?

One friendly message after a completed job, asking honestly for feedback, is welcomed far more often than it annoys — and it's how your Google profile fills with the reviews that win future jobs.

How many calls did you miss last week while working?

Each one was probably a booked job — for someone else. Free consultation; we'll show you the fix.
