FAQ

Questions business owners actually ask

My work comes from referrals. What does a website add?

Referrals verify before they call — that search is part of the referral now. A credible site converts more of the referrals you're already getting, and books them straight to your calendar.

Is automated follow-up appropriate for professional relationships?

Written properly, it reads as diligence, not marketing: 'Following up on the proposal — happy to walk through any questions.' That's the message you'd send anyway, sent reliably.

Client matters are confidential. Where does the CRM fit?

The CRM handles business development — inquiries, calls, proposals, onboarding logistics. Confidential working files stay in your practice tools. Clean separation.

Can it handle retainers and recurring clients?

Yes — recurring check-ins, renewal reminders, and periodic value touches keep retainer relationships warm without manual tracking.

What does a prospect find when they search your name?

Try it now. If the answer isn't 'a credible, current website,' the free consultation is worth 30 minutes.
